Bananas are giant herbs. The “trunk” is a pseudostem built from leaf sheaths.
You have 2 care actions this week. Aim for moist, fertile, well-drained soil and protect those broad leaves from hard wind.

Bonus Study: SHADE//MUSA

This is an educational game, not a calendar. Time is compressed so you can learn the relationships: sun + steady moisture + drainage + potassium + wind shelter + sucker management.

The healthier the mat becomes, the cooler and fuller its music becomes. Listen to stress as well as meters.
