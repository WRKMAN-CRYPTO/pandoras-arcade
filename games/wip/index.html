GRID//BREAKER

Deep below the ruins, arithmetic monsters guard the fracture grid. You cannot defeat them with one giant calculation. Split the numbers, awaken each chamber with its partial product, then combine the four results for the finishing blow. The grid is your weapon.
1. SPLIT
43 becomes 40 + 3. 25 becomes 20 + 5.
2. GRID
Multiply every part by every part.
3. CHARGE
Solve each partial product.
4. COMBINE
Add the four results to land the final hit.
BOSS FLOORS: every fifth depth unlocks the next place value. Bosses attack in two three-seal waves. The large combat buttons stay active while the expanded grid shows where each place-value strike lands.
